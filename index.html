Hifdh Helper

A hifz tracker that helps you plan your Quran memorisation and revision, day by day.

About Hifdh Helper

Hifdh Helper is a free Quran memorisation (hifz) planner and tracker. Enter what you already know and your goal, and it works out what to do on each study day, with the exact surahs and page numbers in the 604-page Madinah mushaf.

Three parts to every day

  • New hifz: the new pages or lines to memorise today, down to the exact line, spread over your study days to reach your finish date or weekly amount.
  • New revision: recent new hifz on a spaced repetition schedule. By default it is revised every study day for 21 days, then 3, 7, 14 and 30 days after that.
  • Revision: everything older, revised in a full cycle, every 30 days by default. Each day’s list starts from the first pages not yet revised in the cycle.

Also included

  • See which surahs are revised this cycle, juz by juz, and tap any surah you revised to mark it.
  • Hard pages come back the next day, 3 days later and 7 days later, on top of normal revision.
  • Modes for being sick, travelling or on your period, so your plan fits your day.
  • Memorise forward, or backwards surah by surah from Juz Amma.
  • A daily reading tick with a streak, for reading pages not yet memorised.
  • Juz-by-juz progress, your current pace and a projected finish date.
  • Several people on one device, for a family or a class, each with their own colour theme.

Questions

What is Hifdh Helper?

Hifdh Helper is a free web app for memorising the Quran. You enter what you already know and your goal, and it works out how much new hifz, new revision and older revision to do on each study day, with the exact surahs, pages and lines.

What is the difference between new revision and revision?

New revision covers what you memorised recently, on a spaced repetition schedule so it sticks: every study day for 21 days by default, then 3, 7, 14 and 30 days later. Revision covers everything older, revised in a full cycle, every 30 days by default.

How are the daily amounts worked out?

Choose a finish date or an amount to memorise each week, in pages or lines, and the date you start. New hifz is spread over the days of the week you study. Older revision is your older pages divided across your revision cycle.

What happens if I do more or less than my target?

For new hifz the week has a set amount, so doing extra one day makes the following days lighter. For revision the daily target stays the same, and each day’s list starts from the first pages you have not revised yet in this pass, so nothing is repeated or missed.

Can I mark revision of surahs that were not on today’s list?

Yes. On the Revision card, tap any surah you revised that is not done yet, or enter page numbers. It counts towards the day, each page is counted once, and you can see which surahs are done in the current cycle, juz by juz.

What if I am sick, travelling or on my period?

Choose it in Settings. Sick pauses everything while you rest, though you can still add anything you do. Travelling pauses new hifz and keeps everything else. On your period you choose, for revision, reading and new hifz, whether to keep it as normal, stop it, or do more each day.

Can I memorise from the end of the Quran, such as Juz Amma?

Yes. Mark what you already know, then choose to go backwards. New hifz then goes surah by surah towards Al-Fatihah, learning each surah from its first page, and skips anything already known.

Can I track more than one person?

Yes. Add a person for each child or student. Each person has their own goal, schedule, logs and colour theme.

Which mushaf does it use?

The 604-page Madinah mushaf with 15 lines to a page, and 30 juz of about 20 pages each.

Is it free, and where is my data stored?

It is free and needs no account. Your plan and logs are saved in your browser on your own device.
