Hifdh Helper

A hifz tracker that plans your Quran memorisation and revision, day by day.

About Hifdh Helper

Hifdh Helper is a free Quran memorisation (hifz) planner and tracker. Enter what you already know and your goal, and it works out what to do on each study day, with the exact surahs and page numbers in the 604-page Madinah mushaf.

Three parts to every day

  • New hifz: the new pages or lines to memorise today, spread evenly to reach your finish date or weekly amount.
  • New revision: recent new hifz on a spaced repetition schedule. By default it is revised every study day for 21 days, then 3, 7, 14 and 30 days after that.
  • Revision: everything older, revised in a full cycle, every 30 days by default.

Also included

  • Hard pages come back the next day, 3 days later and 7 days later, on top of normal revision.
  • A daily reading tick with a streak, for reading pages not yet memorised.
  • Juz-by-juz progress, your current pace and a projected finish date.
  • Revised different pages from the ones planned? Mark them as revised and they count towards the day.
  • Several people on one device, for a family or a class.

Questions

What is the difference between new revision and revision?

New revision covers what you memorised recently, on a spaced repetition schedule so it sticks. Revision covers everything older, revised in a full cycle.

How are the daily amounts worked out?

Choose a finish date or an amount each week, in pages or lines. The remaining pages are spread over the days of the week you study. Older revision is your older pages divided across your revision cycle.

Can I change the revision schedule?

Yes. In Settings you can change the every-day period, the spaced review gaps, the length of the older revision cycle, or switch to revising the last few days of new hifz every day.

Can I start from the end of the Quran?

Yes. Mark any juz or page range as already known, then choose where new hifz continues from and whether it goes forward or backwards, for example from Juz Amma.

Is it free, and where is my data stored?

It is free and needs no account. Your plan and logs are saved in your browser on your own device.
